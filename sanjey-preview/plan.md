## Design
**Movement:** contemporary print manga / risograph editorial, not a dark sci-fi dashboard. **Principles:** recruiter-first readability; original expressive ink drawings; bold asymmetric pages; scroll motion that supports rather than obscures copy. **Color:** paper is the neutral canvas, black is the structural ink, yellow/red or purple/silver are sparing story accents. **Layout:** irregular framed panels, staggered overhangs, scenic full-bleed hero and villain pages, horizontal desktop fight strips; stacked, readable mobile pages. **Signatures:** imperfect halftone screentone, an ink brush progress strip/page-corner turns, impact kanji-like decorative glyphs (decorative only), speed-line burst titles. **Interaction:** every animation has a stable readable final state; explicit Lite/Read switches, keyboard-accessible characters and debug state. **Animation:** a scrubbed GSAP ScrollTrigger pinned orbital opening, layered SVG CSS transforms and Framer Motion provide motion without WebGL; independent paper-page fallbacks; a 90 BPM staggered accent pulse; page/ink wipes, controlled perspective parallax, velocity that eases back, and opt-out/reduced-motion suppression. **Type:** keep the display face for impactful titles, a high-readability Inter body and JetBrains Mono labels; black type on paper is the default. **Brand essence:** the recruiter-ready engineer who ships the punchline as working software; direct, inventive, self-aware. **Voice:** "One punch. Zero failures." and "Garou hunts heroes. I hunt hallucinations." **Mark:** preserve the angular HA/Sanjey registry monogram; rough black ink edge with yellow stamp. **Signature color:** #FFD400.
